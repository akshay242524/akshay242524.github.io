Hello!

I'm AKSHAY

I'm a software engineer who likes working where firmware meets hardware. For three years at MSD, I brought up LTE and 5G NR cellular modems on ARM-based RTOS platforms, writing the C and C++ that ties the upper and lower layers of the protocol stack together. Across two modem generations, we cut the time from first boot to production-ready by 47%.

Bring-up usually started before the chip existed. I worked on pre-silicon emulation and FPGA platforms, writing validation scripts that caught 38% of our firmware integration defects before first silicon showed up. Once real hardware arrived, the hardest bugs sat right on the line between firmware and hardware, and I spent a lot of hours with JTAG, LLDB, and trace logs working them down.

What I want next is a team that brings new modems to life, from first boot all the way to the devices people carry around every day.

General Deep Learning Model for Detecting Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is a common and potentially sight-threatening complication of diabetes. Early detection and accurate diagnosis of DR are crucial for effective treatment and prevention of vision loss. In recent years, deep learning techniques have shown remarkable success in various medical imaging tasks. This research paper presents a general deep learning model designed specifically for detecting diabetic retinopathy in retinal images. The proposed model leverages convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to automatically extract relevant features from retinal images and classify them into different stages of DR severity. Experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed model in accurately detecting DR, showcasing its potential for clinical deployment.
